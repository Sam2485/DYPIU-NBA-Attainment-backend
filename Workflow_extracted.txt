2. Role-Based Operational Workflow
IQAC
 Has access to all Schools and their academic records.
 Views consolidated academic, attainment, ATR and approval records.
 Acts as the top-level institutional oversight layer.
Director
 Works within the assigned School.
 Creates Departments under the School.
 Assigns the HOD for each Department.
 Creates/oversees Programmes under the Department.
HOD
 Manages the Department's Programmes.
 Assigns Programme Coordinators to the relevant Programmes.
 Initializes and manages Batches, e.g. BE Computer Engineering 2025–29.
 Creates and maintains PO, PSO and PEO for the Programme.
 Creates/manages Courses for the applicable Batch/Semester.
 Reviews and approves Programme ATR.
Programme Coordinator
 Works only within the assigned Programme.
 Manages Programme-level setup.
 Sets PO/PSO targets.
 Receives/uses course-level attainment results across the Programme.
 Creates the final Programme ATR for the completing Batch.
 Submits Programme ATR to HOD for verification.
Course Coordinator
 Works within the assigned Course and Batch/Semester scope.
 Defines Course Outcomes (CO).
 Sets target for each CO.
 Creates CO–PO/PSO competency mappings.
 Uploads End Semester Marks.
 Uploads Course End Survey.
 Runs/initiates CO attainment calculation.
 Completes Course ATR using actual attainment versus CO target.

7. Approval Workflow
Course ATR: Course Coordinator → Submission → configured reviewer → Approved / Revision Required.
Programme ATR: Programme Coordinator → HOD → Approved / Revision Required. Every status transition is
stored in Approval History.
Approved ATRs are treated as historical snapshots and should not be silently overwritten by later calculations.
8. Core Design Rules
 Academic Year is the primary UI filter.  Batch is the central historical scope.  Every assessment/attainment
record must retain Batch context.  Completed Batches remain queryable.  Calculation Runs preserve historical
results.  Programme ATR is created only when required programme-level attainment and target data are
available.
