System Architecture
Academic Structure
School
■
■■■ Department (Optional)
■
■■■ Programme
■
■■■ Batch
■■■ Semester
■ ■■■ Course
■ ■■■ Course Outcome (CO)
■■■ Students
Academic Year is the primary user-facing selector. Batch is maintained in the background for batch-wise tracking, student records, ATR
continuity and longitudinal reports.
Programme defines duration and total semesters. Semester year is derived from semester number: 1–2 = Year 1, 3–4 = Year 2, 5–6 =
Year 3, 7–8 = Year 4.

Attainment Calculation Methodology
Direct CO Attainment
· Direct CO Percentage = (Students attaining the configured threshold / Total students) × 100.
· The percentage is converted to a configured attainment level.
· The stored Direct CO Attainment used in the final calculation is the attainment level, not the raw percentage.
Indirect CO Attainment
· Weighted Score = (Slight × 0.33) + (Moderate × 0.66) + (Substantial × 1.00), using the active configuration.
· The weighted result is converted to a percentage and then to an attainment level.
· The stored Indirect CO Attainment used in the final calculation is the attainment level.
Overall CO Attainment
· Overall CO = (Direct Level × Direct Weight) + (Indirect Level × Indirect Weight).
· Default example: Direct 80%, Indirect 20%.
· Example: Direct Level 3 and Indirect Level 2 → 2.8.
· Overall Course CO Attainment = average of Overall CO values for all COs of the course.
PO / PSO Attainment
· PO Competency Attainment = average of mapped Overall CO Attainment values.
· PO Attainment = aggregation of PO competency attainments.
· PSO Competency Attainment follows the same mapped-CO logic.
· PSO Attainment = aggregation of PSO competency attainments.
Programme Attainment
· Programme Direct PO/PSO Attainment is aggregated from course-level results for the Programme and Batch.
· Programme Exit Survey provides indirect PO/PSO evidence.
· Final Programme Attainment = Direct × configured Direct Weight + Indirect × configured Indirect Weight.
· Final programme results are compared with configured targets for ATR.

Key Design Rules
· Academic Year is the main selector shown to users.
· Batch is maintained in the background for historical tracking, student records, reports and ATR continuity.
· Department remains optional.
· Programme defines duration and total semesters.
· Semester year is derived from semester number.
· HOD manages active, completed and archived Batches.
· HOD creates Courses with name, code, semester and credits.
· Students are resolved by PRN during direct-assessment upload and linked to Batch.
· Course End Survey is used for Indirect CO Attainment.
· Programme Exit Survey is used for Indirect Programme PO/PSO Attainment.
· Attainment calculations are versioned by Calculation Run.
· Historical calculations and approved ATR snapshots are not modified by later calculations.
· Reports are snapshots and retain their generation parameters.
